Your training plan should be yours. Athletickle generates programs on your phone and stores your programs, workout logs and settings locally. You do not need an Athletickle account.

## What stays on your device

The programming engine runs locally. We do not operate a training-data backend that receives your programs or workout logs. You can follow your saved training without uploading your sessions to us.

Local storage also has a tradeoff: there is no cloud backup or cross-device sync. Exporting a program lets you share its text; it is not a restorable backup of the app.

## What still needs a connection

Exercise demonstrations open external video links. Those providers handle your visit under their own policies. Store purchases and subscription checks also need connectivity; premium access has a three-day offline grace period.

Subscriptions run through Apple or Google. Payment details are handled by the store, not by an Athletickle payment form.

## The website and Discord are separate

The website uses Glyphex for cookieless analytics. It helps us understand visits to the public site; it is not a feed of your app workout history. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the current details.

Discord is our community and beta communication channel. Joining is separate from using an app. If you choose to post a screenshot, a training log or feedback there, you are sharing it with that audience under Discord’s policies. Only share what you want others to see.
