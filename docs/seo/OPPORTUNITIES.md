# SEO and pSEO opportunities review

Prepared 24 September 2026 against `main` at `3aaf6e8` (clean working tree). Scope: search-engine findability (Google, Bing), AI-assistant findability (`llms.txt`, crawler policy), structured data, internationalization URLs, social previews, deploy hygiene, CI, and programmatic SEO (pSEO) page families. This is a review, not an implementation. Nothing in the site changed as part of it.

Decisions already taken by the founder: technical SEO first, new page families later; English is the first-class locale, the other three follow.

Every recommendation must stay inside [the claims register](../marketing/CLAIMS.md). Both apps are pre-launch.

---

## A. Where the site stands today

The foundation is better than most pre-launch sites. Every route is prerendered to static HTML at build time (`src/entry-server.tsx` lists the routes, `scripts/prerender.ts` writes `dist/**/index.html`, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`). Each page has a title, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags, generated by `renderHead()` in `src/seo/meta.ts`. The blog has hreflang for four locales with `x-default` on English. Four 1200x630 social cards exist. `scripts/check-marketing.mjs` smoke-tests four routes after a build. Deployment is Vercel with clean URLs.

What is missing, roughly in order of how much it matters:

| # | Gap | Why it matters |
|---|-----|----------------|
| 1 | Homepage translations (FR/DE/ES in `src/i18n/marketingCopy.ts`) have **no URL**. Locale comes from localStorage or browser, so crawlers only ever see English at `/`. | The best-written copy on the site is invisible to three markets. Also, hreflang on the blog points at locales that have no localized home. |
| 2 | No `llms.txt`, no `llms-full.txt`, no AI-crawler policy in `robots.txt`. | AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) increasingly answer "what app should I use for X" from crawled context. The site has claim-safe, well-structured copy that is ideal for this and it is not being offered. |
| 3 | Structured data is thin: `Organization` + `WebSite` on `/`, `BlogPosting` on posts, nothing else. FAQ content exists on `/`, `/parkour`, `/strength` but has no `FAQPage` markup. No `SoftwareApplication`, no `Person` for the founder, no `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization.logo` is the favicon, `dateModified` equals `datePublished`. | Rich results and entity understanding. Google needs to learn that "Athletickle" is an organization with two mobile apps and a named founder with credentials. |
| 4 | No Search Console or Bing Webmaster verification, no IndexNow. | Nothing tells Google or Bing the site exists; nothing notifies them when pages change. Pre-launch is exactly when indexing lead time matters. |
| 5 | Sitemap has `lastmod` only for blog posts; no RSS/Atom feed; static pages report no freshness. | Crawl scheduling and feed-based discovery. |
| 6 | Social cards only for 4 pages. Blog posts fall back to the brand card, and `progressive-overload-guide` uses `/previewofprogram.png` as its cover, which `check-marketing.mjs` explicitly bans on marketing pages. No `og:image:width/height/alt`, no `twitter:site`. | Click-through from shared links; a banned screenshot leaking through the blog. |
| 7 | Icons: single 256px PNG favicon. No `favicon.ico`, SVG, `apple-touch-icon`, web manifest or `theme-color`. `vite.svg` is leftover. | Brand presence in SERPs (Google shows favicons), iOS home screen, Bing. |
| 8 | `vercel.json` has no headers (cache, security) and no redirects (`/en/blog` currently 404s on purpose but could 301 to `/blog`). www/apex canonicalization is unverified. | Duplicate-URL hygiene and Core Web Vitals. |
| 9 | No CI. `check-marketing.mjs` is manual, covers 4 of 20 routes, and has no SEO assertions. | Regressions ship silently. |
| 10 | Client bundle over 500 kB (per `docs/marketing/VALIDATION.md`); `public/logo_preview.png` is 1.1 MB (unreferenced), two other unreferenced screenshots ~350 kB each. | Core Web Vitals, and dead weight in the deploy. |
| 11 | Content depth: 3 posts, ~300 to 500 words each, no topic hubs, no glossary, no internal-link structure beyond post to edition. Posts link "join the beta" to `/#download`. | Nothing to rank for beyond the brand name. |
| 12 | `useHead()` only updates title/description/canonical on SPA navigation; OG, hreflang, JSON-LD and `html lang` go stale. | Minor: crawlers read the prerendered HTML. Matters for share previews after in-app navigation. |
| 13 | `SITE_URL` fallback is marked provisional in `src/seo/config.ts`. | Every canonical and hreflang depends on this being final. |

## B. Technical SEO opportunities (phase 1, recommended first)

Ordered by impact-to-effort. Each item names the file(s) it would touch so an implementation plan can be written straight from this.

### B1. Give the localized homepage real URLs
- Add `/fr`, `/de`, `/es` as prerendered routes rendering `Design10` with the pinned locale. English stays at `/`. `x-default` points at `/`.
- Extend `localeFromPath()` and `swapLocaleInPath()` in `src/blog/paths.ts` so the language switcher navigates between `/`, `/fr`, `/de`, `/es` rather than only flipping state. `LocaleSync` in `src/App.tsx` then respects the URL on the homepage, and browser-language detection only applies on first visit to `/` (offer a non-blocking banner or switcher hint rather than a redirect; auto-redirect by `Accept-Language` is a known hreflang anti-pattern).
- Add hreflang alternates to `landingMeta()` and a `landingMeta(locale)` signature in `src/seo/meta.ts`.
- Edition and About pages stay English but should carry `hreflang="en"` + `x-default` self-references for consistency.
- Native-speaker review remains a prerequisite before promoting these URLs (per [the release checklist](../marketing/RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md)), but indexing them costs nothing.

### B2. `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`
- Generate both in `scripts/prerender.ts` from the same route list, so they can never drift from the sitemap.
- `llms.txt` (spec: llmstxt.org): H1 site name, one-paragraph blockquote summary, then sections "Editions", "Founder", "Guides" (blog), "Legal", each a bullet list of `[title](absolute URL): one-line description`. Mark non-English blog URLs under an "Optional" heading.
- `llms-full.txt`: concatenated markdown of the edition copy (from `src/data/marketing.ts`), founder bio, shared FAQ, and every English blog post body. Keep it under the claims register; it is literally the text an AI will quote.
- Add AI-crawler stanzas to the generated `robots.txt`: explicitly `Allow: /` for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Bingbot, CCBot. Point to both sitemap and `llms.txt` (non-standard but harmless comment line).

### B3. Structured data
- `FAQPage` on `/`, `/parkour`, `/strength`, built from `editions[x].faq` and `sharedFaq` in `src/data/marketing.ts`. The `<details>` markup already renders the same text, which is what Google requires.
- `SoftwareApplication` (subtype `MobileApplication`) for each edition on its page: `name`, `applicationCategory: HealthApplication`, `operatingSystem`, `offers` with `price: 0` and a description of the first free block, `isAccessibleForFree: true` for the first block. Omit `aggregateRating` (no ratings exist; fabricating is a manual-action risk).
- `Person` for Mika on `/about` with `jobTitle`, `alumniOf` (University of Jyväskylä), `knowsAbout`, and `Organization.founder` pointing at it. Use `@id` anchors so the same node is referenced from `BlogPosting.author`.
- `BreadcrumbList` on blog posts and edition pages.
- `Organization`: switch `logo` to `athletickle-website-logo.png`, add `sameAs: [Discord invite]` and any social profiles once they exist.
- `BlogPosting.dateModified`: derive from `git log -1 --format=%cs -- <file>` at prerender time (Bun can spawn git), or add an optional `updated` frontmatter field. Same source feeds sitemap `lastmod` for every route, not only posts.
- Add a `WebPage` node with `inLanguage` on every page to make locale explicit for entity extraction.

### B4. Search engine registration and push
- **Google Search Console**: verify by DNS TXT (survives redeploys) or by a `<meta name="google-site-verification">` rendered from a `VITE_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` env var in `index.html`. Submit `sitemap.xml`. Founder action; the code side is one env var.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import from Search Console (one click) or `msvalidate.01` meta the same way. Bing feeds DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, and ChatGPT search.
- **IndexNow**: generate a key once, commit `public/<key>.txt`, add `scripts/indexnow.mjs` that POSTs changed URLs to `api.indexnow.org` after deploy (Vercel deploy hook or a GitHub Action on push to main). Bing, Yandex, Naver, Seznam consume it; Google does not, so Search Console remains necessary.
- Fix `SITE_URL` fallback once the domain is final, and set `VITE_SITE_URL` in Vercel so preview deployments don't advertise production canonicals (or, better, emit `noindex` on preview deployments via `VERCEL_ENV`).

### B5. Sitemap and feeds
- `lastmod` on every URL (from git, see B3). Drop `changefreq`/`priority` (ignored by Google).
- `blog/feed.xml` (Atom) per locale, `<link rel="alternate" type="application/atom+xml">` in the blog head. Feeds are also a discovery channel for AI crawlers and newsletter tools.
- Sitemap stays a single file until it exceeds ~1,000 URLs; no index needed now.

### B6. Social images
- Extend `scripts/social-cards.py` to render a card per blog post from its title (already Pillow-based; committed PNGs, not build-time). Store under `public/social/blog/<slug>.png`.
- Change the `progressive-overload-guide` cover in all four locales to its generated card; remove `previewofprogram.png`, `createprog_snip.png`, `logo_preview.png` and `vite.svg` from `public/` (none are referenced in `src/`).
- Add `og:image:width`, `og:image:height`, `og:image:alt`, `twitter:site` (once a handle exists) to `renderHead()`.

### B7. Icons and manifest
- Generate `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon.svg` if the mark exists as vector, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), and `icon-192/512.png` from the existing 256px PNG via the Pillow script. Add `site.webmanifest` and `<meta name="theme-color">`. Google requires the favicon to be reachable and a multiple of 48px to show it in results.

### B8. Vercel hygiene
- `vercel.json` `redirects`: `/en/blog` and `/en/blog/:slug` to `/blog...` (301), trailing-slash already handled.
- `headers`: `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` for `/assets/*` and `/social/*`; `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy`. Keep CSP out of scope until the glyphex tracker's needs are confirmed.
- Confirm in the Vercel dashboard that `www.athletickle.com` 308-redirects to the apex (or vice versa) and that the redirect target matches `SITE_URL`.

### B9. CI and SEO assertions
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: install with Bun, `bun run build`, `node scripts/check-marketing.mjs`, and a new `scripts/check-seo.mjs`.
- `check-seo.mjs` iterates every route in the sitemap and asserts: exactly one H1; title 15 to 60 chars; description 70 to 160 chars; canonical equals sitemap `loc`; every hreflang target exists in `dist/`; every JSON-LD block parses and has `@type`; `og:image` file exists and is 1200x630; no banned phrases from the claims register; `llms.txt` lists every English sitemap URL. Use Google's Rich Results Test manually on one page per type after the first deploy.

### B10. Performance quick wins
- Measure first: `bunx vite-bundle-visualizer` on the client build. The likely culprit is `motion`; if only a handful of animations use it, swap to CSS or lazy-load it per page with `React.lazy`.
- Delete the unreferenced images (B6). Add `loading="lazy"` and explicit `width`/`height` to below-fold images to avoid CLS.
- Preconnect to `glyphex.io` or, better, load the tracker after `load` rather than `defer`.

### B11. `useHead()` completeness
- Update `og:*`, `twitter:*`, hreflang links, JSON-LD and `document.documentElement.lang` on navigation. Small change in `src/seo/useHead.ts`; makes in-app share previews correct.

## C. On-page and content opportunities (phase 2)

These do not need new page families and are claim-safe because they reuse claims-register language.

- **Title tags** currently lean on brand ("Athletickle Parkour | Structured Training"). Query-led variants convert the same page into a ranking asset: "Parkour Strength Training Program App | Athletickle Parkour", "2-Day Full-Body Strength Program App | Athletickle Strength". Keep the em-dash homepage title but front-load the category.
- **H1/H2 vocabulary**: the edition pages never say "program", "app" or "plan" in a heading. Search demand is phrased "parkour strength program", "strength training app for beginners", "2 day full body program". Headings can say this without any new claim.
- **Internal links**: blog CTAs go to `/#download`. Point them at the relevant edition page with descriptive anchor text, and link edition pages back to the two relevant posts ("Read how RIR cues work"). Add a "Related guides" block to `BlogPost.tsx`.
- **Founder page as an E-E-A-T asset**: `/about` is the credibility page. Add the `Person` schema (B3), link it from every `BlogPosting.author`, and add a one-line author box under each post. Google's helpful-content guidance rewards a visible, credentialed author.
- **Blog index by topic**: a `tags` field exists in frontmatter but has no page. `/blog/tag/<tag>` pages are cheap (route + meta) and give the site its first hub pages.
- **Blog depth**: the three posts are 300 to 500 words. The two training posts are on genuinely searched topics (progressive overload, RIR) where competing pages are 1,500 words or more. Expanding them with worked examples from the app's own logic (add/hold/reduce cues, planned deload) is content the founder is uniquely qualified to write.

## D. Programmatic SEO opportunities (phase 3, deferred by decision)

Recorded so the option is on the table when the founder is ready. Each family is scored on search demand, claim-safety, and data readiness.

| Family | URL pattern | Pages | Seed data | Demand | Claim risk | Readiness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Exercise library** | `/exercises/<slug>` | ~258 | `exercises.json` in the app repos (258 rows, 255 demo links). Not in this repo. | High and durable ("how to do X", "X muscles worked") | Low if each page states which block types use it and links to the external demo rather than hosting technique claims | Needs the data copied in, plus per-exercise 80 to 150 words. Biggest win, biggest editorial job. |
| **Glossary / concepts** | `/glossary/<term>` | 15 to 30 | RIR, RPE, deload, mesocycle, block, progressive overload, minimal effective dose, contrast training, autoregulation, roadmap... | Medium, high intent, evergreen | Very low: definitions plus "how Athletickle uses it" | Ready now; content is mostly in existing copy and the in-app training guide. Recommended first pSEO family. |
| **Program option pages** | `/strength/<emphasis>-<days>-days`, `/parkour/<frequency>` | 10 to 20 | Options in `src/data/marketing.ts` (12/24 weeks, 2-day mode, muscle/strength/upper/lower emphasis, jump-focused, contrast) | Medium ("2 day full body program", "upper lower split 4 days") | Medium: every page must repeat the "first block free, 21-day gate, pre-launch" qualifications and must not promise durations or results | Ready once templates and one claims review per page exist. |
| **Comparison pages** | `/compare/<competitor>` | 5 to 10 | Manual | High intent | High: the claims register forbids unverified competitor superiority or pricing; pages must be strictly factual feature tables | Defer until launch; do last. |
| **Localized editions/about** | `/fr/parkour` etc. | 3 pages x 3 locales | Existing English copy | Medium | Low | Blocked on native-speaker review (release checklist). |

Guardrails for any family: every generated page must pass the same `check-seo.mjs` assertions, carry a unique description, link up to its hub and sideways to 3 to 5 siblings, and be excluded from the sitemap while marked `draft`. Thin pages (under ~120 words of unique text) should be `noindex` or not built at all; Google's scaled-content policy targets exactly the "258 near-identical pages" pattern.

## E. Measurement

- Search Console (Google) and Webmaster Tools (Bing) are the only reliable index and query data. Wire both before any content push so there is a baseline.
- Keep the glyphex tracker for visits. Do not add Google Analytics; the privacy-first post and privacy policy are a positioning asset.
- Weekly: indexed page count, impressions per edition page, top queries, hreflang errors, Core Web Vitals (Search Console "Page experience"). Add these to the weekly measurement hour in [the launch kit](../marketing/README.md).
