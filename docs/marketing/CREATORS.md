# Creator and coach collaboration kit

Relationship-led beta outreach. No offers have been sent, and no payment, endorsement, partnership or commission is assumed.

## Personal introduction draft

Hey [name] — I’ve been building Athletickle, bringing my programming experience into an app. There are two versions: complementary strength/power work for parkour, and gym training for strength and muscle.

I’d value your honest take on [relevant edition]. The first four-week block is free. Would you be interested in trying a test build when it’s ready and telling me where the plan or app falls short? No obligation to post. I’m keeping the first group small so I can respond properly.

Here’s what it does: [athletickle.com/parkour or /strength]. We’re coordinating the beta on Discord: https://discord.gg/PrXwF4QSuf

Use actual relationships and personalize the reason for fit. Sign as Mika, Founder of Athletickle. Do not name coaching clients, teams or athletes or use details that identify them. Start with 3–5 people; one follow-up after 7–10 days at most if the relationship supports it. Do not automate mass messages.

## Parkour one-page brief

**Audience:** parkour athletes who want structured complementary strength and power training and have gym access.

**Promise:** give your parkour training a strength-and-power roadmap.

**Three things to show:** input parkour frequency; inspect the planned blocks and first session; open an exercise demonstration or eligible pre-save swap.

**What it is not:** a parkour technique course, a climbing app or a standalone sprint scheduler.

**Offer:** first complete four-week block free; later blocks subscription-based with 21-day unlock pacing. Pre-launch access only when a tested build is available.

**Suggested story:** “How I fit strength work around parkour practice.” Try the first block, discuss its structure with the founder, report your experience honestly.

**CTA:** explore https://athletickle.com/parkour and join the beta Discord.

## Strength one-page brief

**Audience:** gym users who want a structured strength/muscle plan; include people who prefer two training days per week.

**Promise:** give your strength training a direction.

**Three things to show:** select the two-day mode or a regular frequency; choose muscle/strength emphasis; show logged add/hold/reduce cues after real use.

**What it is not:** a kg prescription engine, automatic form checker or equipment-free program.

**Offer:** first complete four-week block free; later blocks subscription-based with 21-day unlock pacing. Pre-launch access only when a tested build is available.

**Suggested story:** “What changes when I plan a block instead of choosing today's exercises?” Show the roadmap and explain the difference between logging cues and block-end feedback.

**CTA:** explore https://athletickle.com/strength and join the beta Discord.
