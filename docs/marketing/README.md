# Athletickle launch kit

Prepared 23 September 2026. Both apps are pre-launch. This kit is ready for founder review; no community posts, invitations, store submissions or deployment have been sent.

## What is implemented

- Benefit-led homepage in English, French, German and Spanish.
- `/parkour`, `/strength`, `/about` in English, with distinct metadata and 1200×630 social cards.
- Working Discord recruitment links; no mailing list or new app analytics.
- Edition-specific feature claims, free-block explanation, subscription pacing and offline limitations.
- Revised blog product claims across existing translations.
- Obsolete screenshots removed from visible pages. The replacement roadmap graphic is explicitly an illustration, not a native screenshot.

## Use this kit

1. Read [claims and evidence](CLAIMS.md) before writing promotional copy.
2. Use [Discord setup and messages](DISCORD.md) to configure the community.
3. Send the relevant brief from [creator outreach](CREATORS.md) personally; record results in `cohort.csv` and `channels.csv`.
4. Publish the [four-week content calendar](CONTENT.md) at a sustainable pace.
5. Use [store copy](STORE-COPY.md) after verifying the release build and actual store availability.
6. Complete [release and capture checks](RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md) before larger creator exposure.

## Six-week working schedule

| Week | Founder work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review copy and coaching bio; review local website | Accurate product pages and founder story |
| 2 | Configure Discord; prepare install instructions for actual builds | Start-here flow and two creator briefs |
| 3–4 | Invite 20 Parkour and 10 Strength testers; approach 3–5 known creators/coaches | Small, supported beta cohort |
| 5–6 | Weekly Q&A; inspect activation issues; gather voluntary feedback | Prioritized fixes and authentic early stories |
| Four weeks after each tester starts | Ask about the completed block | Permissioned case-study candidates |

Budget five hours per week: content 2h, relationships 1h, Discord 1h, measurement/review 1h. If website or build preparation exceeds this, extend the calendar rather than advertise an unready build.

## Measures and decisions

Targets are internal planning goals, not advertised promises: 30 recruited testers, 20 self-reported first sessions and 10 substantive feedback conversations. Record weekly totals per edition.

Website visits come from the current website analytics where available. Discord invite counts show joins, not installs. Installs and training milestones are voluntarily self-reported. Do not infer paid conversions or retention from a click, and do not merge training data with audience tracking.

Once there are 20 tester responses, if fewer than half have generated a program or completed a first session, pause wider creator invitations and investigate the onboarding issue. Expand the next cohort after five independent visitors can explain the offer, installation instructions have been exercised, and first-session blockers are resolved. A threshold is an operating rule, not proof of product efficacy.
