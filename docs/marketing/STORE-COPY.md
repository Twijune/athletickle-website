## Athletickle Parkour

**App name:** Athletickle Parkour

**Subtitle:** Strength & power for parkour

**Short description:** Plan complementary strength and power training around your parkour practice.

**Description:**

Give your parkour training a structured strength-and-power plan.

Tell Athletickle Parkour how often you practise parkour, how many complementary sessions you want, and your resistance-training experience. See a roadmap of three or six four-week blocks before starting your first block.

- Follow prescribed sets, reps, effort and rest.
- Explore jump-focused blocks, contrast work and speed-style lifting sessions in eligible programs.
- Review and swap eligible exercises for equivalents before saving.
- Open linked exercise demonstrations and the in-app training guide.
- Keep programs and logs locally without an Athletickle account.
- Use the end-of-block check-in to inform the next block.

Your first complete four-week block is free, including its scheduled deload. Later blocks require a subscription and a minimum 21-day wait before the next block unlocks, including for subscribers.

Gym access is assumed. This app programs complementary training, not technical parkour practice. Demonstrations and store operations need connectivity; premium access has a three-day offline grace period. There is no cloud backup or cross-device sync.

## Athletickle Strength

**App name:** Athletickle Strength

**Subtitle:** A plan for strength & muscle

**Short description:** Structured strength and muscle training, with a two-day reduced-volume option.

**Description:**

Give your strength training a direction.

Choose your weekly frequency, experience and training emphasis. Athletickle Strength plans three or six four-week blocks, with progression and a scheduled deload in each block.

- Bias your roadmap toward muscle mass, maximal strength or both.
- Choose an upper- or lower-body emphasis, or keep the distribution balanced.
- Use minimal effective dose for two reduced-volume full-body sessions a week.
- Review and swap eligible exercises before saving.
- Log performance for cues to add load, repeat it or ease back.
- Keep your programs and logs on your phone without an Athletickle account.

Your generated block is ready to follow without logging every set. Logged cues suggest a direction, not a kilogram number. The end-of-block check-in informs the next block rather than rewriting your current workouts.

Your first complete four-week block is free. Later blocks require a subscription and a minimum 21-day wait before the next block unlocks, including for subscribers.

Gym access is assumed. Demonstrations and store operations need connectivity; premium access has a three-day offline grace period. There is no cloud backup or cross-device sync.
