# Four-week founder content calendar

One weekly topic, reused as a short founder post, a product walkthrough and a Discord prompt. English first. Publish only when the linked page and any demonstrated build are available. These are ready-to-edit drafts, not scheduled or published posts.

## Week 1 — Strength around parkour

**Post:** “Parkour practice is already training. The complementary work should account for it. Athletickle Parkour asks how often you practise and how many complementary sessions you want, then builds a strength-and-power roadmap. I’m looking for a small first group to try it and tell me what needs work. We’re coordinating the pre-launch beta on Discord: athletickle.com/parkour.”

**Walkthrough:** enter an actual test profile → show its generated roadmap → open the first session. Explain that the app programs complementary work, not the technical parkour practice itself.

**Discord prompt:** What makes strength training hardest to fit around your parkour week: choosing exercises, planning sessions or keeping a consistent schedule?

## Week 2 — What a block looks like

**Post:** “A plan is easier to follow when you can see where it is going. Athletickle starts with a 12- or 24-week roadmap, then builds the detailed training one four-week block at a time. The first block is free and includes a planned deload. Both apps are pre-launch; meet me and the first testers at athletickle.com.”

**Walkthrough:** show the sequence separately from the first block's four weeks. Do not imply that all future workouts are unlocked for free. Mention the 21-day pacing gate when explaining continuation.

**Discord prompt:** Looking at your first block, what do you understand immediately—and what needs an explanation?

## Week 3 — Two days is an option

**Post:** “Some weeks leave less room for the gym. Athletickle Strength has a two-day reduced-volume option: two full-body sessions, with progression and deloads still in the plan. Choose it when creating the program; it stays with that program. No promise of a magic workout length. Just a clear option to explore. athletickle.com/strength.”

**Walkthrough:** show the two-day option and the generated program. Never compare fabricated before/after results or imply it is a live weekly toggle.

**Discord prompt:** For two-day training, which matters most to you: clarity, exercise selection, or being able to see the next block's direction?

## Week 4 — Feedback has a place

**Post:** “There are two different kinds of feedback in Athletickle Strength. Workout logs can give you a direction for the next load: add, repeat or ease back. The check-in at the end of a block informs the next block. Those are different from rewriting every workout in real time. Want to see how the plan works? athletickle.com/strength.”

**Walkthrough:** show a progression cue from actual logged test data and separately show the block-end questionnaire. Label demonstration data. Don't claim Parkour has Strength's load cues.

**Discord prompt:** Which would help you understand the plan better: examples of RIR, the deload week, or how the next block is chosen?
