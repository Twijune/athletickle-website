## Small-server structure

- `#start-here`: read-only introduction and next steps.
- `#announcements`: read-only build availability and release changes.
- `#training-chat`: Parkour and Strength conversation in threads.
- `feedback`: one forum, tagged Parkour/Strength and Setup/Bug/Question/Idea.
- Opt-in roles: Parkour, Strength, Beta updates. Android/iOS are optional self-selected roles.

Avoid a channel for every feature. Use one scheduled 30-minute founder Q&A weekly at a time announced in Europe/Helsinki and UTC. Keep feedback asynchronous; no promise of immediate coaching or individual plan review.

## Check-in posts

**After access:** Were you able to install and generate a program? Which step, if any, blocked you?

**After the first session:** Did you complete a first session? What instruction or screen was least clear? Logging every set is not required to take part.

**After four weeks:** Did you finish the block? What helped you follow it, and what got in the way? Would you choose to continue? Why?

**Testimonial permission:** Would you be comfortable with me quoting your feedback on the website? I’ll send the exact quote and the name/handle I would display for your approval first. Saying no has no effect on beta access.
